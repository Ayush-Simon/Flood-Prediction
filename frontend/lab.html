How close it was on this data

Only rows where the real depth later is known. RMSE: typical miss in inches, lower is better. R² near 1 is good, 0 is no better than the average. “Highest in 40 min” is the deepest water in the next 40 minutes, whenever it comes: the models judge this much better than the exact minute (on the 2026 test storms R² 0.55 against 0.11).

Flood warnings on your data

A flood is caught if any alarm came in the 40 minutes before the depth first reached 1 inch; one continuous alarm counts once, and it is false if no flood started within 40 minutes of it. Alarm levels were tuned on the 2026 test storms.
